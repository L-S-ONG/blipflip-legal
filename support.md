# Blipflip Support

Need a hand with your video diary? Contact us for questions, bug reports, feedback, or account help.

## Get in touch

**Email: [labratrayray@gmail.com](mailto:labratrayray@gmail.com?subject=Blipflip%20Support)**

To help us understand a problem, please include:

- Your phone model and iOS or Android version.
- Your Blipflip app version, if available.
- What you were trying to do, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
- An optional screenshot, with personal details and private conversations hidden.

**Never send your password, verification codes, payment-card details, or private videos.** If account help is needed, contact us from the email associated with your Blipflip account where possible.

## Quick troubleshooting

### I cannot record a clip

Check that Blipflip has camera and microphone permission in your phone's settings. Hold your phone sideways in landscape orientation, then hold the recording button. If it still does not work, close and reopen the app and contact us with the details above.

### A clip, shared moment, or recap will not load

Check your internet connection and try again. Sharing and recap rendering need an internet connection. If the issue continues, tell us which screen is affected and whether you see an error message.

Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage as a troubleshooting step unless you have saved anything you want to keep elsewhere; some content may be stored only on your device.

### I need help with sign-in

Use the same sign-in method you used when creating your account. If you cannot get back in, email us with the sign-in method and the error message. Do not include passwords or verification codes.

## Account and data deletion

To request deletion in the app, open **Profile → Delete account** and read the confirmation before proceeding. Save anything you want to keep first.

If you cannot access your account or need help with a data-deletion request, email [labratrayray@gmail.com](mailto:labratrayray@gmail.com?subject=Blipflip%20Account%20Deletion) with the subject **Blipflip Account Deletion**. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on the request. See the privacy policy for information about data handling.
